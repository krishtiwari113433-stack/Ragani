The seven swars

SaShadjaReRishabhGaGandharMaMadhyam PaPanchamDhaDhaivatNiNishad

Sa and Pa are fixed. Re, Ga, Dha and Ni can be komal (flat), and Ma can be tivra (sharp). In the notation here, a lowercase letter is komal and M′ is tivra Ma.
